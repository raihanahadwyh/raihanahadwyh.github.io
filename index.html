Featured project

Controlling Traffic Congestion in Urbanised City Using Agent-Based Modelling

Tools: QGIS · GAMA · Python Type: GIS-integrated simulation Published 2023

The problem

Urban traffic congestion is difficult to study directly — road networks, driver behaviour, and infrastructure all interact at once. The project set out to model that interaction in a way that could be tested against different congestion-control strategies before they're applied in the real world.

The approach

I built a GIS-integrated agent-based simulation framework representing road networks, traffic infrastructure, and individual vehicle movement. Traffic-related parameters and road characteristics were incorporated into the model, and vehicle interactions were simulated separately under peak and non-peak conditions to compare traffic flow behaviour.

The result

The simulation outputs were analyzed to identify traffic patterns and support a data-driven understanding of urban mobility and congestion. The methodology, modelling framework, and findings were documented and published in a peer-reviewed paper.
